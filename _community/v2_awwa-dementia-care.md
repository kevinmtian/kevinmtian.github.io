I volunteer with AWWA’s dementia and senior-care community in Singapore, supporting companionship, feeding assistance, and meaningful engagement activities for seniors living with dementia. Through these interactions, I have learned how patient, person-centred care can help preserve dignity, connection, and emotional well-being for residents whose daily lives are affected by cognitive decline.

I also initiated and hosted fundraising campaigns on Giving.sg to support AWWA’s community and eldercare work. This experience has strengthened my commitment to contributing to Singapore’s ageing society, and deepened my interest in how technology and AI can support caregivers, improve volunteer coordination, and make community care more sustainable.
